# CommUnity AI

AI-Powered Women Safety and Smart Travel Platform

---

## Problem Statement

Women face safety challenges while traveling:

- Unsafe routes
- Lack of trusted companions
- Emergency response delays
- Limited real-time monitoring

---

## Proposed Solution

CommUnity AI provides:

- AI-powered risk detection
- Smart buddy matching
- SOS emergency support
- Real-time journey tracking
- Trust-based community travel

---
